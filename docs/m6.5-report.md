## Beyond the plan

- The dock folds to a strip of pictures and opens on a click on a tab; a dock narrower than 24 rem names only the open tab.
- `viewer.tools.shortcuts()` lists the bound keys, so a toolbar can show them.
- Series and measurement rows have hover and selected states.

## Not done, or not proven

- **No visual regression suite.** The look was checked by eye in a browser at one width. Screenshots are M8, and a narrow-screen check with real screenshots is still owed.
- **A host's own panels have no picture** until `PanelDefinition` gets an `icon`.
- **Touch input** and a touch-sized toolbar are not designed.
- **The palettes were chosen by me**, not by a designer. They pass the contrast tests, and that is all that is claimed.
- **Chromium only**, as before.
